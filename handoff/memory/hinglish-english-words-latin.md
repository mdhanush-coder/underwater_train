In Devanagari Hinglish VO scripts, write English loanwords in English (Latin script): reactor, neutron, uranium, design, grid, megawatt, April. Hindi words and Indian names stay in Devanagari. NUMBERS are spoken in English, written as English words in Latin (e.g. one hundred eighty nanometer, twenty twenty-five, four thousand five hundred crore), no Hindi number words (updated 2026-11-12, missile-chips video; this replaces the older numbers-in-Devanagari rule from BrahMos). The bare article एक is fine.

**Why:** user corrected the Thorium script (2026-11-11) after it had न्यूट्रॉन, रिएक्टर etc. in Devanagari.
**How to apply:** every Hinglish script and TTS text. Related: [[xu-hindi-voice-sameer-b]].
