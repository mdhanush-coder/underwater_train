From the Gaganyaan brief (2026-11-09), user's standing visual rules for XplainedUnseen docs:
- Mix licensed real photos (ISRO/PIB/public-domain/CC first, verify licence on the original host), 2.5D parallax of photos, Blender 3D, diagrams, MG. Not all-CG.
- Handwritten engineering annotation style: off-white / muted gold / occasional warning-orange strokes, drawn stroke by stroke, attached to real components (tracked in 3D, screen-space on photos). Only meaningful labels, never decoration. Real verified measurements.
- Keep an asset attribution + licensing register (URL, creator, licence, modifications, usage status). No news footage, YouTube clips, or stock without permission; images with unclear rights are reference-only.
- Per-scene plan table: narration, visual source, reference URL, copyright, camera, handwritten elements, facts, animation, sound, transition.
- Separate completed tests vs announced plans vs unconfirmed details.
- No final render until visual treatment, permissions and accuracy are reviewed.

**Why:** wants a credible, premium engineering documentary, not generic 3D slides.
**How to apply:** every XU video, with [[brahmos-no-text-boxes]] and [[brahmos-palette-charcoal-gold]].
