Decided 2026-11-13 after analysing two Indian Spyverse videos (3iNtYdsba44, HbDkAwgwUOA). Applies from the NEXT video, not BrahMos.

Formula:
- Cold open in present tense with date/place, no intro; first image by 0:03.
- Short one-fact sentences (~9-10 words, ~3 s each), ~150-160 wpm.
- Reversal hook within ~25 s ("From up there, nothing... But under the dunes...").
- Story wrapper for hook and each chapter opener; explanation inside it, in the same short sentences.
- Chapter title cards; hedges ("One account says...") for disputed claims.

Shot rhythm:
- New shot (angle or scene) on each new idea; shots ~4-5 s.
- One callout on the spoken word ~1-2 s into each shot gives a 2-3 s visual beat.
- Constant slow camera move; never a static hold.
- Few reusable sets, many cameras each, switched by timeline markers (one render job gives many shots).

**Why:** the user wants that hook/retention style; BrahMos script and voice are already approved, so it's not retrofitted.
**How to apply:** at the scripting stage of the next video. Keep the rhythm but not their look: keep [[brahmos-palette-charcoal-gold]], [[brahmos-no-text-boxes]], [[xu-3d-realistic-colours]], and [[chips-smooth-transitions]] for scene changes (hard cuts only for angle changes inside one scene).
